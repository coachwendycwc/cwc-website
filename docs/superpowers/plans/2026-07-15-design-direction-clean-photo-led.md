# CWC Design Direction: "Clean & Photo-Led"

**Source of inspiration:** https://unbounded-potential.com/catherine/ (Catherine Wood, Unbounded Potential) — Rafael flagged it as clean + easy. Studied 2026-07-15.

**The core insight:** That site feels clean NOT because it has less padding — it feels clean because **every sparse section has a large photo as its visual anchor + exactly one focal point.** CWC's "empty/thin" sections are *centered text floating with no image to hold the space.* The fix for CWC's whitespace is NOT less padding — it's **adding a visual anchor (photography) to sparse sections.**

---

## What makes the Catherine site work (transferable to CWC)

1. **Lead with LARGE founder photography.**
   - Hero: full-height photo of the founder, text beside it.
   - Bio: half-width photo, quote + story beside it.
   - The person IS the brand. Warm, human, instant trust.
   - **CWC application:** make Wendy as prominent. The About page and section leads should use big, warm Wendy photos — not just the homepage video.

2. **One focal point per section.**
   - Hero = one line + one headline + one paragraph + one photo. Nothing else.
   - No cards/grids/icons/stats competing for attention.
   - **CWC application:** where a section feels empty, don't shrink padding — give it a single anchor (a photo, or one strong element). Sparse + anchored = calm. Sparse + floating text = thin.

3. **Big, quiet type on generous whitespace.**
   - Two-column: text left, image right (or alternating). Simple.
   - Whitespace reads intentional because the photo fills the visual weight.

4. **Warm, soft palette + conversational copy.**
   - Muted blush/cream bg, ONE accent color.
   - First-person: "Hey there, new here? Welcome!" / "Twelve years ago I lived in..."
   - Reads like a person, not a company.

---

## Honest counterpoints (don't blindly copy)

- **Catherine's site is a PERSONAL coach page (one person).** CWC is a FIRM: multi-service, org + individual audiences, case studies, solutions catalog. CWC legitimately needs more structure.
- **CWC is AHEAD in substance:** real solutions grid, testimonials w/ photos, client logos, bento resources, structured service pages. The Catherine site is beautiful but THIN on depth.
- **Some of CWC's density is a feature.** The lesson is targeted: apply photo-led calm to the SPARSE sections, don't strip the substance from the rich ones.

---

## Where this specifically applies to CWC (candidate sections)

Sections that currently feel "thin/floating" and would benefit from a photo anchor or one-focal-point treatment:
- `/resources` hero + "Free Tools & Guides" (centered text, no image → add a Wendy photo or resource visual)
- `/about` — should lead with big Wendy photography (Catherine-style bio block)
- Service-page heroes with lots of bottom padding + no right-column visual
- Any centered-text-alone section

Sections that are FINE as-is (don't touch — they have anchors/substance):
- Homepage solutions grid, for-organizations/for-individuals split heroes (already photo-led), case-studies, testimonials, bento resources featured card.
