Brand Identity Anchor
We Are ✓
Empowering — we activate what's already there
Direct & naming — we say what others won't
Credentialed — 25+ yrs, 500+ leaders, Fortune 500
Warm & invested — we see the whole person
Systemically aware — we name structural barriers
Outcome-driven — promotions, raises, culture change
We Are Not ✗
Savior-coded — implying clients need fixing
Corporate-euphemistic — "diverse talent," DEI buzzwords
Boastful or cold — credential dumps without warmth
Overly therapeutic — we also challenge and push
Politically evasive — pretending challenges are just personal
Vague inspiration — "unlock your potential" with no proof
3 Rules to Live By
1
Always say "women of color." Never "diverse talent," "underrepresented," or "BIPOC" as a catch-all. The specificity is the brand.
2
Validate before you pitch. Name the pain → validate it → offer the solution. "We See You" before the program. Always.
3
Activate, never rescue. Wendy is a guide, not a savior. Copy that implies clients are broken is off-brand — even if well-intentioned.
Tone by Context — Voice stays constant · Tone flexes
Context Formality Energy Depth Key Principle
B2B cold outreach Med-High Medium Low-Med Lead with the retention cost. Pain point first, credentials second.
B2B discovery call Medium Med-High Medium Ask more than tell. Diagnose before prescribing.
B2B proposal / RFP High Medium High ROI + named clients + scoped deliverables + process steps.
B2C social media Low-Med High Low Validate first. "We See You" energy. Short, punchy, specific.
B2C website / programs Medium High Medium Pain point → validation → solution. Never lead with the price.
Keynote / speaking Med-High High Medium Data to open authority. Story to close on emotion.

Copy Examples — Good vs. Off-Brand
✓ On-brand — B2B opening

"Your leaders are leaving. Women of color are among your highest performers — and among the most likely to leave when they're not developed. We help organizations retain them before that talent walks out the door."

✗ Off-brand — generic DEI vendor

"We help organizations build more inclusive cultures and empower diverse talent to unlock their full potential." → Fix: Name the specific audience, specific outcome, and specific cost of inaction.

✓ On-brand — B2C validation opener

"Women of color in the workplace face challenges that are rarely named out loud — but deeply felt every day. You're not imagining it. And you don't have to navigate it alone."

✗ Off-brand — rescue framing

"Let Wendy help you find your voice and step into your greatness." → Fix: "Wendy helps you amplify the leadership voice you've already built." Client has it — Wendy amplifies it.

Never Ever
Start with Wendy's bio before the client's problem
Use "BIPOC" as a default — say "women of color"
Imply clients are broken or need fixing
Use "soft skills" — leadership is not soft
Reference "life coaching" — positioning removed
Pretend challenges are purely personal — name the system
Vague promises without a specific outcome

The RESET Method® — Proprietary Framework

Closes performance gaps by coaching both the leader and the team member simultaneously.

"Doesn't choose sides — it closes gaps."

1
Discovery & Assessment — Separate sessions, both sides, no assumptions
2
Parallel Coaching — Leader on managing, team member on ownership
3
Alignment & Outcome — Mid-point checkpoint + outcome summary

Best for: VP of HR · Directors · HR Business Partners

ROI frame: "Close performance gaps. Retain your talent."

The CWC Teaching Voice Pattern — Use in Any Content That Names a Problem
Step 1: Challenge

Name the dynamic — "The Silent Achiever: you deliver results but no one outside your team knows your name."

Step 2: Root Causes

Name why it happens — cite data, validate experience, don't individualize the problem.

Step 3: The Breakthrough

Provide a practical tool, script, or framework. Always actionable — never just inspiration.
